Innovation & Venture Platforms/Incubation

Incubation

From lab research to market-ready. A platform for early-stage ventures.

Silal × Khalifa University

The IO Incubator’s inaugural cohort, Agri-Robotics 2026, is a collaboration between Silal and Khalifa University to accelerate the commercial readiness of four agricultural technologies.

Teams refine prototypes with open access to greenhouses, open fields, labs and real farm operations, working directly with the farmers who will use them. The goal is to close the commercialisation gap between research and the market with solutions built for local conditions.

Partner
Khalifa University
Cohort
Agri-Robotics 2026
Timeline

Goal
Prototypes to market-ready solutions

Cohort 1

Four technologies, built with Khalifa University.

TouchRIPE

A wearable device that assesses fruit firmness without damaging it, using vision-based tactile sensors. It answers market demand for objective ripeness evaluation and better shelf-life forecasting.

Aim
Less food waste, higher marketable yields
Market
Fruit quality and post-harvest management

PollenMatic

A compact, versatile device for efficient pollination in greenhouse tomato cultivation. The project refines the prototype and validates its performance in real-world conditions.

Target
TRL 6 / CRL 5
Aim
Cost-effective, user-friendly artificial pollination

PlantAI

An AI-powered mobile app that detects leaf diseases across key UAE crops: tomatoes, cucumbers, capsicum and berries. The project validates accuracy across diverse field environments.

Aim
Earlier detection, timely intervention
Next
Readiness for commercial deployment

AerialYield

An autonomous system for yield estimation and fruit ripeness analysis in blueberry cultivation, advanced through real-world data collection, field trials and end-user validation.

Aim
Better yield forecasts, less labour
Crop
Blueberries
Why incubate at Innovation Oasis

Built around the people who will use it.

  • Real testbeds

    Open access to greenhouses, open fields, labs and real farm operations.

  • Farmers in the loop

    Project teams work directly with end users, so solutions are practical as well as scientifically rigorous.

  • Built for UAE conditions

    Designed around local regulatory, environmental and market realities, not adapted afterwards.
