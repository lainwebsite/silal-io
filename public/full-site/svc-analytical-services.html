Our laboratories

Integrated analysis from soil to shelf

  • Soil & Water Laboratory

    Testing for soil, irrigation water, plant tissue, compost, fertiliser and biochar.

  • Crop Health Laboratory

    Identifies plant diseases, pests, nutrient-related issues and physiological disorders.

    Assesses whether planting material can establish a healthy crop.

Soil analysis

Understand fertility, salinity and soil condition.

Soil analysis can assess

  • Electrical conductivity
  • Moisture
  • Soil texture and physical properties
  • Available phosphorus
  • Soluble sulphate
  • Nitrogen
  • Organic carbon
  • Macro- and micronutrients

  • Potential toxic ions
  • Nematodes

How it works

From sample to result

  1. 01

    Define the requirement

    Tell IO what is being sampled, the crop, the question, the number of samples and the timing.

  2. 02

    Confirm the analysis

    IO confirms the tests, sample requirements, price, turnaround and reporting format.

  3. 03

    Collect and submit

    Samples are collected, labelled, stored and transported following the guidance.

  4. 04

    Receive and prepare

    The laboratory logs, identifies, checks and prepares each sample.

  5. 05

    Analyse and review

    Samples are tested using the agreed methods and quality-control procedures.

  6. 06

    Report

    You receive the agreed reporting outputs.

  7. 07

    Interpret and progress

    Where included, IO explains findings and recommends next steps.

Antimicrobial packaging stickers

Result

FAQ

Questions about testing

Which test should I request?

IO can help identify an appropriate test package based on the sample, crop, issue and intended decision.

How long will results take?

Each test has its own turnaround time, which varies by method, sample condition and laboratory capacity.

Are recommendations included?

Some services provide laboratory results only. Interpretation and agronomic recommendations are identified separately in the quotation.

Can IO diagnose a crop problem from a photograph?

Photographs may help with initial triage but do not replace a representative sample and appropriate diagnostic testing.

Can the laboratory support research projects?

Yes. IO can develop project-specific analytical plans, repeat-sampling schedules and controlled or field-testing support.

Is the laboratory accredited?

The Soil and Water Laboratory operates under an ISO 9001-certified management system. ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation is targeted by the end of 2026.
