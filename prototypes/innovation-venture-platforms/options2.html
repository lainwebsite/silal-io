        E · Al Miyah Challenge for Agriculture

        Why it doesn’t work yet, and two ways it can.

        What goes wrong now

        Nine pieces compete in one view: label, title, organiser, status pill, statement, two paragraphs, prize, two problem statements and five category chips. Nothing says which to read first, so a first-time visitor reads none of it.

        What a visitor needs, in order

        1. What is this? A water challenge.
        2. Who runs it and what is at stake? MBZ Water Initiative, AED 8M, 846 teams.
        3. What is Silal’s part? It tests the finalists.
        4. What is being tested? Two problems, each shown further down the page.

        So the fix is

        One message, three facts, then the two problems as doors to the sections that already explain them (the five pilots and CRETA). The long paragraph and the categories become secondary or move down. Both options below follow this order.
