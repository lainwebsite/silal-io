01 Farm Innovation Fund

Farmers drive every stage.

Cohort 1 · 10 finalists · 5 pilots to fund

The Farm Innovation Fund connects real farm problems with a structured path to develop, test and scale practical solutions.

It is open to farmers and farm operators across the UAE, whatever the maturity of the idea. Early concepts and solutions ready for an on-farm trial can both enter. The programme adapts its support to the evidence, readiness and needs of each proposal, and farmers keep ownership of their ideas at every stage.

02 Incubation

From lab research to market-ready.

Agri-Robotics Cohort 2026 · with Khalifa University

The IO Incubator is a platform for early-stage ventures. Its first cohort is a collaboration between Silal and Khalifa University to bring four agricultural technologies to commercial readiness.

Teams refine their prototypes with open access to greenhouses, open fields, labs and real farm operations, working directly with the farmers who will use them. The aim is to close the gap between research and the market with solutions built for local conditions.

Read all FAQs ↗
Does participation require giving up equity?

No. Silal does not take equity as a condition of participation. Selected participants enter into a Participant Agreement with commercial provisions in Silal’s favour, typically a Right of First Refusal on GCC commercial deployment for a defined period post-pilot, revenue participation on partnerships Silal originates, and first-conversation rights on GCC opportunities. Participants retain ownership and global commercial freedom outside the GCC.

Do startups need to be based in the UAE?

No. We prioritize companies committed to building a meaningful presence in the UAE and the broader GCC. Selected startups spend time on-site at Innovation Oasis in Al Ain during the pilot phase, working closely with Silal teams and partners.

How is this different from other programmes?

It is for growth-stage startups with proven technology. Cohorts are small (3 to 5 startups, two cohorts a year), so each startup gets a tailored pilot. Pilots run inside Silal’s actual operating environment with real business units, and successful pilots lead to defined commercial pathways, not just a demo day.

What is the time commitment?

A structured engagement over 3 to 5 months: in-person time plus ongoing collaboration with Silal teams, with time upfront to scope and define the pilot.

Is a pilot or commercial engagement guaranteed?

The platform is designed to enable pilots and commercial opportunities, but outcomes are not guaranteed. Where pilots succeed, Silal works with startups on next steps, including potential commercial agreements or longer-term partnerships.

Al Miyah Challenge for Agriculture

Organised by the Mohamed bin Zayed Water Initiative · Silal as technical partner

Agriculture uses nearly 70% of all water consumed globally. The challenge rewards solutions that cut water use while maintaining or improving yield.

The inaugural competition of the Mohamed bin Zayed Water Initiative is open to innovators, companies, start-ups and researchers worldwide. Solutions must be proven, ready to pilot in the UAE, and transferable to similar climates in the GCC and Sub-Saharan Africa.

The sixth finalist · CRETA Group, UAE

A greenhouse cooled by the ground.

Hot desert air is drawn through underground channels, where stable soil temperatures pre-condition it before it enters the growing space. A dual-shell “Mother Greenhouse” reduces the heat load with minimal energy and no water spent on evaporative cooling.

12–15°Creported temperature reduction in pilot testing
Zerowater used for cooling

Silal builds and commissions it at Innovation Oasis: groundworks for the air channels, the dual-shell structure, instrumentation for temperature, humidity, energy and water, and a full crop cycle. Operating data is shared with ADAFSA for evaluation under local climatic conditions.
