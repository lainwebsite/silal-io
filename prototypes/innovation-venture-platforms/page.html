Al Miyah Challenge for Agriculture

Organised by the Mohamed bin Zayed Water Initiative · Silal as technical partner

Testing at Innovation Oasis · winners December 2026

Agriculture uses nearly 70% of all water consumed globally. The challenge rewards solutions that cut water use while maintaining or improving yield.

The inaugural competition of the Mohamed bin Zayed Water Initiative is open to innovators, companies, start-ups and researchers worldwide. Solutions must be proven and ready to be piloted in the UAE, with results that transfer to similar climates in the GCC and Sub-Saharan Africa.

AED 8 million prize pool, about AED 4 million for first place.

Problem statement A

Sustainable irrigation

Improving sustainable irrigation practices and reducing overall water consumption in agriculture.

Problem statement B

Controlled-environment design

Optimising greenhouse and net house designs, including water used for cooling.

Problem statement B · CRETA Group, UAE

A greenhouse cooled by the ground.

Hot desert air is drawn through underground channels, where stable soil temperatures pre-condition it before it enters the growing space. The dual-shell structure reduces the heat load with minimal energy demand.

12–15°Creported reduction in pilot testing
Zerowater used for cooling

Silal builds and commissions it at Innovation Oasis, runs a full crop cycle benchmarked against a conventional evaporative-cooled greenhouse, and shares the operating data with ADAFSA.

What the testing phase measures

Less water, same or better yield, ready to scale.

  • Water use

    Irrigation volume per crop cycle and per kilogram of produce, against an untreated control.

  • Yield and quality

    Harvested weight, marketable grade and crop health under heat and salinity.

  • Operating performance

    Energy demand, labour and input cost; for the greenhouse, conditions through the summer peak.

  • Scalability

    Cost per hectare, ease of adoption on UAE farms and transfer to similar climates.
