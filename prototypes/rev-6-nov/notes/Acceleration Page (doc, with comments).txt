2. WHY

WHY IO ACCELERATION

From proven technology to commercial opportunity

The UAE offers something most acceleration programs cannot: demanding operating conditions, advanced agrifood infrastructure and direct access to organizations capable of adopting, distributing or commercializing new solutions.

Acceleration connects these assets to mature ventures ready to prove value and grow in the region.

Three primary objectives

Validate

Test performance, reliability and economics under relevant UAE conditions.

Localize

Adapt the technology, value proposition and delivery model for the UAE and GCC.

Commercialize

Build the right route to customers, channels, partners or capital.

3. Who It Is For

VENTURE PROFILE

Built for companies ready to scale

Acceleration is designed for mature ventures with differentiated agrifood or adjacent-sector technologies that are ready for growth into a new market.

Ideal profile

-   Proven product or working system

-   Typically TRL 7 or above

-   Evidence from a relevant operating environment

-   Clear advantage over existing solutions

-   Credible customer and economic value

-   Capacity to deploy and support a UAE pilot

-   Interest in entering or expanding across the UAE and GCC

-   A strong team with the technical and commercial capability to execute

The current platform model targets mature ventures, broadly from Series A to C, but company quality and deployment readiness matters more than funding stage alone.

5. How Acceleration Works

From global pipeline to commercial pathway

Keep the process short and divide it into two distinct parts.

Find and select

1. Source

Identify high-potential technologies through global scouting, applications and partner networks.

2. Evaluate

Assess maturity, differentiation, evidence, UAE relevance, team capability and commercial potential.

3. Match

Connect shortlisted ventures with a relevant problem owner, business sponsor/customer or commercial partner.

4. Plan

Develop a clear testing plan, success measures and commercial hypothesis.

5. Pilot

Deploy the technology and test technical, operational and economic performance.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

Keep this short and founder-focused.

Is this a traditional accelerator?

No. The platform focuses on operational validation, localization, market access and commercialization rather than general startup education.

Is funding guaranteed?

No. Support depends on selection, final pilot scope, budget, milestones and partner participation.

Is procurement guaranteed?

No. The platform generates evidence and commercial engagement, but procurement and other commercial agreements remain subject to partner decisions and approvals.

Does the company need to be based in the UAE?

International ventures are encouraged to apply, provided they are committed and able to deploy, support and commercialize their solution in the region.
